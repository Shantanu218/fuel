      Fills in what you actually ate and burned on average last week.

      How this works: each simulated day takes your maintenance (BMR × activity level) plus exercise, subtracts what you eat, and converts the gap at about 7,700 kcal per kg. As your weight changes, your BMR moves with it (about 10 kcal per kg), so progress slows over time. Day-to-day weight also swings with water, salt and carbs, so read this as a direction rather than a promise.

      Your data

      Your entries are stored only in this browser on this device. To carry them to your other device, save a sync file and load it there.

      Move your data between devices

      Before you switch devices

      Save a sync file and send it to yourself on WhatsApp. On iPhone you can send it straight from the share sheet. On a laptop it downloads.

      On the device you switch to

      Load the newest sync file. On iPhone, tap the file in WhatsApp, then Share, then Save to Files, so you can pick it here.

      Loading combines both devices. If the same entry was edited on both, the newer edit wins, and anything you deleted stays deleted.
